Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

✓ TipDeler stores all data locally on your device. Nothing is sent to any server. We do not collect, store, or share your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

TipDeler does not collect any personal information. All data you enter — venue names, staff names, shift data, and tip amounts — is stored exclusively on your device using local storage.

2. Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your iPhone or iPad using Apple's UserDefaults and device storage. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not transmitted to any server
  • Is not accessible to us or any third party
  • Can be deleted by uninstalling the app

3. Subscriptions & Payments

TipDeler uses Apple's In-App Purchase system for subscriptions. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive, store, or process your payment information. For details on how Apple handles your payment data, please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Analytics & Tracking

TipDeler does not use any analytics tools, tracking pixels, or third-party SDKs. We do not track your usage, behavior, or location.

5. Children's Privacy

TipDeler is designed for restaurant and café managers and is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at support@chmays.app.
